How stress is classified

  1. Stress Index (SI): each stress item is rescaled to 0–1 and the two are averaged:
    SI = 100 × [ (Q2 − 1)/9 + (Q3 − 1)/4 ] / 2  (0 = lowest, 100 = highest)
  2. Under Stress if SI ≥ 50; otherwise Stress-Free.
  3. Why 50? 50 is where both answers sit at the middle of their scales, so SI ≥ 50 means the student's answers lean toward the stressed end on balance. Using two items (an overall rating and a frequency of feeling overwhelmed) makes the classification less sensitive to how one person interprets a single 1–10 scale.

Limitations

Convenience (non-random) sample; self-reported, one-time answers; association is not causation; departments may be unbalanced; possible duplicate submissions because the survey is anonymous.
